Each Element

<c-each> is the element form of c-each, the list counterpart of <c-series>. Its items render in its place, here between a static first and last <li>. Its settings are the attribute form's without the c-each- prefix (mode, idkey, expression), and the list is given in items. The HTML parser doesn't allow <c-each> inside <select> or tables; use the c-each attribute there.

count = {{m().items.length}}
